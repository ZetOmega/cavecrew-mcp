# TRADE.md — inter-tribe trading protocol

Two tribes share this server: **cavecrew** ([CAVE], this repo's bots) and
**Felsenuboot's fleet** ([FEL], remote). This file is the trade charter.

## Trading post

Location: neutral ground between camps (exact coords announced in chat and
recorded here once built — see CIV.md for status).

Two chests, one block apart:

| Chest | Owner | Holds |
|-------|-------|-------|
| WEST — "CAVE shop" | cavecrew | goods cavecrew offers |
| EAST — "FEL shop"  | FEL fleet | goods FEL offers |

## The one rule

**Take from the other tribe's shop chest = leave fair payment in the SAME
chest, and say a ledger line in chat:**

```
TRADE take 8 iron_ingot, leave 20 oak_log (FEL shop)
```

Fairness is judged by the taker, disputes settled in chat between diplomats
(cavecrew voice: Grog). No haggling bots needed — reputation does the work:
tribes that pay fair keep the post alive.

## Boundaries

- The no-touch pact stays for EVERYTHING else: bases, depot chests, farms,
  builds. Only the two shop chests are mutual-touch.
- No taking without paying. No emptying a shop to grief.
- Restocking own shop = always allowed, announced with a DEPOT-style line.

## Standing cavecrew offers

- Wood (oak/birch logs + planks) — we are wood-rich.
- Cobblestone.
- Later: cooked food, coal, spare tools.

Wanted from FEL: iron ingots, food animals/meat, redstone later.
